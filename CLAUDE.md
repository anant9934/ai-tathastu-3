## 🏛️ Mandatory Anti-Slop Design Doctrine

Every interface built in this project must look like it was designed by a world-class human product designer, **never like the statistical average of an AI model**.

### The 7 Anti-Patterns (BANNED)
1. **No Generic "SaaS Template" Defaults:** Never default to a centered headline over three lines with a tiny pill badge on top, followed by a 3-column icon card grid.
2. **No Lazy Gradients & Floaty Shadows:** Avoid generic purple-to-blue gradients and oversized, washed-out drop shadows (`shadow-2xl` on everything). Use crisp 1px borders, subtle tint-mapped shadows, and intentional backgrounds.
3. **No Unintentional Emoji Icons:** Never use emoji characters (🚀, ✨, 💡, 🔥) as interface icons. Use precision SVG icons (Lucide, Radix, or Phosphor).
4. **No Sluggish / Floaty Animations:** Micro-interactions must be snappy (100ms – 150ms). Modals, drawers, and sheet transitions must be 200ms – 300ms max. Never animate `width` or `height` directly; animate `transform` and `opacity`.
5. **No Wall-to-Wall Grey Text:** Do not make secondary text an unreadable washed-out grey. Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA contrast minimums (4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for large text).
6. **No Raw Hardcoded Hex/Pixel Values:** Every color, spacing, radius, and duration must use design tokens or Tailwind theme classes (`bg-surface-elevated`, `text-foreground-muted`).
7. **No Incomplete Component States:** Every interactive component must specify all 8 states:
   `default` · `hover` · `focus-visible` · `active` · `disabled` · `loading` · `empty` · `error`
